Very inspiring and positive book ! There are so many things to say about this book, but if you are looking for understanding the flow, techniques to achieve it, how to trigger it on a regular basis, for you and more interesting to achieve it in a collective way, this book is for you.<br />The book made me learn a lot about hormones roles, how to customize daily routines and why. There are a lot of techniques, but just by picking somes and applying new ones progressively, there is a big room for progress.<br />I could learn about myself, why sometimes some - yet minor - activites did help (without being sure they did), and how I'll be sure to use them, even when very tired and finding these ones "useless".<br /><br />If you want to find energy, grit, and have already noticed - or want to develop - curiosity, this book is for you.
