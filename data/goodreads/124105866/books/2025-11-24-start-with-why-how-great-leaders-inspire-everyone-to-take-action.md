I discovered this book after having seen youtube videos. Then I choose to give it a try, and I was really not disappointed.<br />This book is very easy to read, full of real life examples, highly inspirative. By multiplying examples it makes it easy to remember the method and how to be able to remember the "method".<br />I use this as a reflex in many areas, for my personal projects, in my job with my team, and with my students at University. This helps a lot in better choosing where to put efforts, how to question things around us.<br />For people who love to create products, are creative, and love to perform, this book is a must-read, very stimulating and positive.
